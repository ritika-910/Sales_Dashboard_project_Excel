# Excel_Sales_Dashboard_project
Interactive Excel Sales Dashboard built on 2,707 orders across 17 European countries (2026). Used 10 PivotTables, slicers, KPI cards, linked charts and a country map to track revenue, COGS, gross profit, margin and orders. Insights: 645K revenue, 45.9% gross margin; Western Europe, Camping and Online Store lead, with sales peaking in June

Key Points

Data: 2,707 orders, 17 countries, 4 regions, 5 categories, 20 products (Jan-Dec 2026)
Tools: 10 PivotTables, linked charts, KPI cards, a country map and slicers
Interactive: Slicers filter the whole dashboard with one click
KPIs: Total Revenue, COGS, Gross Profit and Total Orders
Revenue and profit: 645K revenue and 296K gross profit, a 45.9% gross margin
Top region: Western Europe (198K), followed by Central Europe (191K)
Top category: Camping (199K), then Hiking and Travel
Best channel: Online Store, about 40% of revenue (260K)
Trend: Sales peak in June (69.5K)
Top product: Summit Backpack 45L by gross profit
